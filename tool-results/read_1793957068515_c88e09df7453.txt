    79→## A3. Business rules (port these exactly)
    80→
    81→### A3.1 Assessment cycle and weighting
    82→- A report cycle combines **two components**: **Component 1 = 25%** (Monthly test) and **Component 2 = 75%** (Mid-Term exam, or Terminal exam).
    83→- **Component names come from data.** Form 2 used "Opening Test" (25%) and "Monthly Test" (75%). Model components as named items with weights, not as fixed columns.
    84→- **Subject total ("the 100% rule"):**
    85→
    86→| Component 1 | Component 2 | Subject total | Shown |
    87→|---|---|---|---|
    88→| present | present | `round(c1×0.25 + c2×0.75, 2)` | c1, c2, total |
    89→| present | blank | c1 (counts 100%) | c1, "–", total |
    90→| blank | present | c2 (counts 100%) | "–", c2, total |
    91→| blank | blank | **subject left off the card** (no row, no chart bar, not counted in "Subjects taken") | — |
    92→
    93→- **Component average** = sum of the marks entered ÷ **the number of marks actually entered**, not the number of subjects. The TERMINAL Excel divided by all subjects; that was a bug.
    94→- **Overall score** = `avg(c1)×0.25 + avg(c2)×0.75` when both exist. If only one component exists, its average counts 100%. Round to 2 dp.
    95→  - This can differ slightly from the mean of the per-subject totals when the student sat different subjects in each component. This is a **known quirk; confirm the rule** (Part C).
    96→- **Layout switch:** if **nobody** in the class has any component-2 mark, hide the component-2 column, label component 1 as "100%", and hide Best Improvement.
    97→- Display marks as integers. Display totals and averages with 2 decimals (e.g. "75.00").
    98→
    99→### A3.2 Grading scales
   100→**Primary (Grades 1–6): letter grade only, no points, no division**
   101→
   102→| Grade | Range | Descriptor |
   103→|---|---|---|
   104→| A | 81–100 | Outstanding |
   105→| B | 61–80 | Very Good |
   106→| C | 41–60 | Good |
   107→| D | 21–40 | Satisfactory |
   108→| E | 0–20 | Can do better |
   109→
   110→**Secondary (Forms, including Vocational), NECTA CSEE style**
   111→
   112→| Grade | Range | Descriptor | Points |
   113→|---|---|---|---|
   114→| A | 75–100 | Outstanding | 1 |
   115→| B | 65–74 | Very Good | 2 |
   116→| C | 45–64 | Good | 3 |
   117→| D | 30–44 | Satisfactory | 4 |
   118→| F | 0–29 | Can do better | 5 |
   119→
   120→- Thresholds apply to decimal values, so 80.99 is a B for primary.
   121→- Each subject's grade comes from its **weighted total**, not from the exam mark alone.
   122→- **Use "Outstanding" for A everywhere.** Some legacy templates said "Excellent"; that was a bug.
   123→
   124→### A3.3 Division (secondary only; fewer points is better)
   125→- Points = the sum of the **best 7 (lowest) subject points**, computed from the combined subject grades. There is **no core-subject rule**.
   126→
   127→| Division | Points | Label |
   128→|---|---|---|
   129→| I | 7–17 | Distinction |
   130→| II | 18–21 | Credit |
   131→| III | 22–25 | Merit |
   132→| IV | 26–33 | Pass |
   133→| 0 | 34–35 | Fail |
   134→
   135→- **Per-class "No Division" flag:** Form 2 Vocational uses the secondary scale but shows no points and no division.
   136→- **Gap text:** "Highest division achieved" for Division I; otherwise "N point(s) from Division X". Example (fake): 19 points (Division II) → "1 point from Division I".
   137→- **Unresolved edge case:** a student with **fewer than 7 graded subjects**. The legacy system printed nothing. Needs a decision (Part C).
   138→
   139→### A3.4 Card-level derived values
   140→| Value | Rule |
   141→|---|---|
   142→| Top / Strongest subject | the subject with the highest total (on a tie, the first in class subject order) |
   143→| Best Improvement (primary only) | the subject with the largest (c2 − c1); a subject with only one mark counts as 0; printed as `+x.xx` |
   144→| Subjects Taken | the number of subject rows printed |
   145→| Subjects Passed | rows whose grade is not E or F. **Never hard-code "All Passed"** (legacy bug) |
   146→| Class average / class top (chart) | per subject, over the class: the mean and the max of each student's weighted total; students with neither mark are excluded |
   147→| Position / rank | **Not on the card today.** It was removed from an older version. The school's raw results files still contain POS. Needs a decision (Part C) |

   149→### A3.5 Comments, attendance, conduct, signatures
   150→| Item | Current behaviour |
   151→|---|---|
   152→| Class teacher comment | One overall comment per student, **typed by a person, never generated**. Clean the whitespace, soft hyphens and zero-width spaces. Printed in italic between gold quote marks |
   153→| Comment check | Warn (do not block) when a comment says "not enrolled in / not taking / does not study X" while X has marks |
   154→| Per-subject comments, parent comment | TERMINAL only. Make them optional per cycle |
   155→| Attendance | **Legacy card: absent. ERP card: INCLUDED (decided 2026-10-24).** Read from the ERP's daily registers; rules in A3.8 |
   156→| Conduct, gender, admission number, photo | Not on the card |
   157→| Signatures | Three slots: **Class Teacher · Academic Dean · Principal**. Image or blank line. **No approval workflow today:** if the signature file exists, the card counts as signed. The ERP should add a real sign-off |

   159→### A3.6 Mark status codes (from the analytics pack and the device policy)
   160→| Code | Meaning |
   161→|---|---|
   162→| (number 0–100) | a real mark; 0 is a real zero |
   163→| `ABS` | absent |
   164→| `NR` | no result where one was expected |
   165→| `NA` | subject not taken (optional subject) |
   166→| `PENDING` | no learner in the class has a mark for this component yet |
   167→| `DISQ` | **disqualified for exam malpractice**: counts as zero, the parent is notified the same day, a disciplinary record is created (Digital Devices Policy JSS/DD/POL/2025/001 §8) |
   168→| `INVALID` | unusable input (text, more than 100, less than 0); must never be saved |
   169→
   170→Missing scores are **never** filled in by estimation.

   172→### A3.7 Analytics rules (for class, HOD and management dashboards)
   173→- **Pass:** primary A–C (41+); secondary A–D (30+). **Quality pass:** A–B (primary 61+, secondary 65+).
   174→- **Performance Index:** grade index A=100, B=75, C=50, D=25, E/F=0, averaged.
   175→- **Traffic lights (configurable):** Performance Index green ≥62.5, amber ≥50 · Risk Index (% of learners in Tier 2/3) green ≤10%, amber ≤20% · Completion green ≥95%, amber ≥85% · Subject Health (% of class-subjects rated Strong/Stable) green ≥70%, amber ≥50%.
   176→- **Subject rating** (class-subject, n≥5): Strong / Stable / Watch / Priority Intervention / Critical, from the mean against the pass/quality bands and the % below pass.
   177→- **Support tiers:**
   178→  - **Tier 3 Intensive:** overall below pass, or 3+ subjects below pass.
   179→  - **Tier 2 Targeted:** 2 subjects below pass, or Maths/English/Kiswahili below pass, or close to pass with a failed subject, or a strong decline while below quality pass.
   180→  - **Tier 1 Monitor:** everything else that is flagged.
   181→  - Review dates: Tier 3 in 14 days; the others at the next assessment.
   182→- **Change categories:** Strong improvement ≥+10 · Moderate ≥+5 · Stable ±5 · Moderate decline · Strong decline ≤−10.
   183→- **Moderation signals:** component mean shift ≥10, pass-rate shift ≥15, SD ≥20 or ≤5, IQR ≤5, ≥40% below pass, rank correlation (Spearman) <0.5, parallel-stream gap ≥10.
   184→- **Privacy:** named-learner views are "CONFIDENTIAL — academic use only". The public view has no names and **no group smaller than 5**. Intervention bands are never shown to parents.

   186→### A3.8 Attendance on the report card (new in the ERP; the legacy cards had none)
   187→
   188→**Source data already in the ERP.** Read it; never rebuild attendance.
   189→- **Daily register (roll call):** one mark per student per school day, per stream. There is **no per-lesson register**.
   190→  - Table `attendances`. Filter `context = 'daily_roll_call'`, `attendable_type = 'person'`, `attendable_id = student's person_id`, with `section_id` and `occurred_on` (date).
   191→  - `status` is a typed enum: `present | absent | late | excused`. Plus `arrived_at` (late arrivals) and `note`.
   192→  - Unique: one mark per student, per day, per stream.
   193→- **`roll_calls`:** one row per stream per day. Only **counted** registers are valid: live registers, plus catch-up ("backfill") registers that have been **approved** (`RollCall::scopeCounted()`). A pending or declined catch-up register must not count.
   194→- **Corrections** (`attendance_corrections`, approved) already rewrite the `attendances` row, so the card just reads `attendances`. `attendance_changes` is history only.
   195→- **Non-school days:**
   196→  - Weekends.
   197→  - `public_holidays`. Attendance uses this table; the older `tz_public_holidays` is used only by SEN.
   198→  - `school_holidays` (date ranges).
   199→  - **No-register days**: `events` with `event_type = 'no_register'` and a scope of school, unit, class or section. An absence on a no-register day is automatically turned into **excused**.
   200→- **Existing helpers to reuse without editing:** `Services/Attendance/SchoolDayCalendar::isSchoolDay()`, `RegisterCalendar::reasonFor(section, date)`, and the counting pattern in `AttendanceDashboardController`.
   201→- **What does not exist yet:** a per-student or date-range summary, a term date table, and any portal attendance view. The report card needs a **new read-only aggregator**.
   202→
   203→**Computation for student S over the cycle's date range [from, to]** (`attendance_from`/`attendance_to` on the report cycle, defaulting to term start → report date):
   204→1. Marks = the student's `daily_roll_call` rows in range, queried **by person, not section**, so a mid-term stream transfer still counts. Skip soft-deleted rows. Call `withoutGlobalScope('class_teacher')`: the `ClassTeacherScoped` scope returns nothing for a queued job with no logged-in user.
   205→2. Drop marks on days whose register does not count (catch-up register not approved).
   206→3. **Possible school days** = weekdays, minus public holidays, minus school holidays, minus no-register days for the student's stream. Load holidays and events **once** for the range, never one query per day.
   207→4. **Not recorded** = possible school days with no counted mark. **Show it separately; never count it as absent.**
   208→5. Counts: Present, Late, Absent (= unexcused), Excused. **Days attended = present + late.**
   209→6. **Attendance rate** = (present + late) ÷ (present + late + absent + excused) × 100, rounded to 1 dp. This matches the ERP attendance dashboard: late counts as attended, and excused stays in the denominator. Confirm the formula with the school (C13).
   210→
   211→**What the card shows** (compact section "ATTENDANCE", after the Performance summary):
   212→| Field | Example (fake) |
   213→|---|---|
   214→| Days attended / school days recorded | 58 / 62 |
   215→| Attendance rate | 93.5% |
   216→| Late arrivals | 3 |
   217→| Absent (unexcused) | 2 |
   218→| Excused | 2 |
   219→| Not recorded (only if > 0) | 1 day not recorded |
   220→| Period covered | 6 July – 4 September 2026 |
   221→
   222→- **Rating text** (configurable thresholds; suggested defaults): ≥95% "Excellent attendance" · 90–94.9% "Good attendance" · 80–89.9% "Attendance needs improvement" · <80% "Attendance is a concern". Text plus colour, never colour alone. **Bilingual EN/SW on the parent PDF and portal.**
   223→- Parent-facing wording: say "Absent", not "Unexcused". Never show notes, reasons or correction history on the card.
   224→- **Sensitivity:** attendance is a regulated typed field (CLAUDE.md rule 1), and attendance work needs tests (rule 7). Today `audit_logs.sensitive_category` has **no 'attendance' value**, so attendance reads are not audited (a known platform gap). Card reads are already audited as 'grades'. Adding 'attendance' to the enum is a platform migration for Deo (C12).
   225→- **Scope guard:** an aggregator placed in `backend/app/Services/Academic/` (e.g. `ReportCardAttendanceSummary`) that only reads attendance stays inside the allowed paths. Editing `Services/Attendance/*`, attendance models, `routes/academic.php` or the audit enum does not (C1, C12).
